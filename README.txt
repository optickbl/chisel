CHISEL - your training app, outside Claude

Put these files online once, then install Chisel on your iPhone's home screen.
After the first visit it works offline, and your log stays on your phone.

Free hosting with GitHub Pages (works from iPhone Safari):
1. Sign up at github.com.
2. Tap + > New repository. Name it chisel, keep it Public, tap Create.
3. Tap "uploading an existing file", choose every file in this folder, then Commit changes.
4. Open Settings > Pages. Under Branch pick "main" and "/(root)", then Save.
5. After about a minute, open https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/chisel/ in Safari.
6. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen.

Moving your log from the Claude version:
- In Claude: open Chisel > Vault > Export save file.
- In the new app: Vault > Load a save file, and pick that file.

Updating later: upload all the files from the new folder over the old ones. The app picks it up the next time it opens online.
On a computer you can also just double-click index.html.
